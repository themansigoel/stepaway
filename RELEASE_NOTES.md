# Release Notes — Step Away (Mac)

## Version 1.7.0 (Build 25) — Released: August 20, 2026

### 🌐 Multi-Language & Global Expansion
- **Full Italian & Korean Localization:** Complete native experience with localized menus, settings, activity charts, exercise instructions, and alerts (`it.lproj`, `ko.lproj`).
- **Comprehensive 9-Locale Coverage:** English, German, French, Spanish (Spain & Mexico), Italian, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese.

### ⏱️ Smart Breaks & Ergonomics
- **Enhanced Break Scheduling:** Smarter reminder cycles with fine-tuned interval options designed to blend seamlessly into modern Mac workflows.
- **Guided Neck & Eye Stretches:** Rapid ergonomic micro-breaks and posture reminders to relieve desk tension and prevent neck strain.
- **Improved 20-20-20 Rule Timer:** Refined gentle notifications, smooth countdown transitions, and instant previews for Spine Align and Blink Guide.

### 🛡️ User Privacy & Performance
- **Guideline 2.4.5 Compliance:** Launch at Login is completely opt-in requiring explicit user consent via General Settings.
- **Onboarding Experience:** Re-enabled clean first-launch walkthrough explaining menu bar operation and customizable timers.
- **macOS Performance & Battery Optimization:** Reduced background idle CPU footprint and optimized energy consumption.
